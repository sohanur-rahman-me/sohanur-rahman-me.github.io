A little about me

Curiosity grounded

I want to connect rigorous engineering work with practical outcomes—whether in a research lab, a production environment, or a future manufacturing technology.

I am completing my B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering at RUET, Bangladesh, with a strong interest in research and industry-oriented problem solving. My current undergraduate research focuses on bamboo–jute hybrid epoxy composites modified with nanoclay, with attention to mechanical performance, thermal behaviour, and moisture resistance.

Alongside research, I’m interested in production planning, lean manufacturing, process improvement, engineering design, and the thoughtful use of simulation and data analysis to improve products and systems.
